Rotate the pieces in your mind before you place them. That is the part that helps.
Why Tetris? Right after something upsetting, your brain spends a few hours "saving" the memory, mostly as vivid pictures. Tetris keeps that same visual part of your brain busy, so the memory gets stored less vividly and pops back into your head less often. You still remember what happened; it just intrudes less. It works best within about 6 hours, for 10 to 20 minutes, while really focusing on rotating the pieces in your mind.
